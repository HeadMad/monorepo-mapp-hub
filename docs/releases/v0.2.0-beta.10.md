## MappHub Shell v0.2.0-beta.10

### Fixed
- The app content overlapped the status bar and the gesture/navigation bar. The WebView now takes system bar insets as padding (`MainActivity`). The CSS `env(safe-area-inset-*)` workaround from beta.9 did not take effect in the WebView and was removed.
- The browser close button could not be tapped because it sat under the status bar. Fixed by the same inset change.
- The console shared the browser's service container, so enabling it could take over the whole screen or squeeze the browser. The console now has its own dock container (`#console-host`) and does not change the browser layout.
- The console can be collapsed with the ✕ button.

### Updated
- Browser mini-app 0.1.0-beta.4, console mini-app 0.1.0-beta.3, launcher 0.1.0-beta.6.
- Market, sample-game and theme-editor mini-apps have their beta.9 safe-area CSS reverted.

### Known limitations
- Not verified on a physical Android device.
- Installed mini-apps are not replaced by a new APK. Update browser, console and launcher from the Market or reinstall them.
